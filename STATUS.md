## Boundaries

The product is a public exhibition, not an engineering backend. Its home artwork is rendered by Qt from native source; surrounding browser chrome follows source styles but is not promised pixel-identical to OS-native decorations on every platform. App interiors are actual captures; no results or processing are fabricated.

No requested source asset is missing. Original PNGs and desktop code remain unchanged. Optimized WebPs preserve the full composition and dimensions; they are not lossless archives. The Empty captures remain references only and are not used as app page substitutes.

`content/shells/`, the default-interface generator and the old layout/wafer/preview runtimes have been removed from active code. Normal builds require only committed content and assets. GitHub Pages remains static, with CNAME unchanged. Local captures and fallback fixtures are under ignored `.qa/native-review/`.
