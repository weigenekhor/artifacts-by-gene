## Review criteria

Silent-film meaning, poster-frame quality, differentiation, safe areas, mature color, composition and actual interaction are separate checks. Every tool needs establishment, development, decisive event and hold. Canvas labels must not collide or clip during intermediate states. Screenshot precision and film geometry are separate quality questions.

Fresh captures and machine-readable outcomes are in ignored .qa/. VALIDATION.md records actual checks and remaining limitations. A passed build is not visual approval.
