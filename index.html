01 / Why ARTIFACTS exists

Some difficulty belongs to the work.

A vast registration instrument works until one region loses its reference. The obstruction is separate from the complexity of the work.

Some doesn’t.

The reference is deliberately repositioned. Alignment returns and holds. The improvement remains while the camera moves on.

A spatial field resolves, then comparison structures align. The camera pulls back to reveal the retained operations. A central substrate turns forward and becomes the real ARTIFACTS homepage.
