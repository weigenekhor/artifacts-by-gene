02 / WHY ARTIFACTS EXISTS

Engineering is
difficult enough.

Some difficulty belongs to the work.

Some doesn’t.

One problem
became a tool.

Then another.

There was no masterplan.
Just work that kept revealing
where it could be better.

Different problems.

The same refusal
to leave friction
untouched.

That became
ARTIFACTS.
