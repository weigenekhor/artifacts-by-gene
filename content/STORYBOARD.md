## One clock and one environment

Native scroll supplies one continuous timeline. A persistent Three.js scene owns the reusable instanced instrument elements, measurement surface, traces and camera. Shared motion tokens separate camera damping, object response and UI timing. Scene transitions interpolate positions and camera poses rather than reset whole scenes. DOM typography follows the same clock with masks and perspective. Real captures stay in the DOM for sharp text. Only images near the active scene are loaded.

On portrait screens, typography occupies the upper field and geometry the lower field. The system view offers one Expedition at a time with all four immediately selectable. Reduced motion composes settled explanatory states and preserves all content. No-WebGL uses a deterministic SVG explanation; no-JavaScript retains story and the complete catalogue. Pointer input is optional; every app and capture is keyboard accessible.
