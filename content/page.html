ARTIFACTS / By GeneBuilt over three years
Behind the work

Gene

Three years. Sixteen applications. None of them started as a product idea.

They started with work that should have been better.

A comparison that took too long. A diagnosis buried in data. A process that relied too much on memory. A task repeated often enough that doing it manually stopped making sense.

I did not accept the workflow simply because it already existed.

If I could make it clearer, faster, harder to get wrong, or easier to repeat, I built a better way and put it to work.

Then I did it again.

Some tools solved one problem and stayed focused. Others grew far beyond the problem that created them. Every one had to earn its place through real use.

Three years later, those sixteen applications are ARTIFACTS.

ARTIFACTS is not a collection of side projects.

It is the standard I refuse to work below.
