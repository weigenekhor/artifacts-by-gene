I built ARTIFACTS over three years, one practical need at a time. It began with tools I wanted beside me in engineering work.

Each one holds a way of working I wanted to keep: a comparison, a check, a clearer path through the evidence. Sixteen applications, shaped by the work that called for them.

The work is personal.
The standard is not.
