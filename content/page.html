02 / WHY ARTIFACTS EXISTS

Engineering is
difficult enough.

Some difficulty belongs to the work.

Some doesn’t.

The friction
wasn’t theoretical.

  1. Reconstructing a lot’s history.Event by event.
  2. Finding the right SPC chart.Level by level.
  3. Rebuilding an engineering report.Again.
  4. Working out the next equipment check.From the last one.
  5. Comparing reactor configurations.Node by node.

I built what
was missing.

Different problems demanded different tools.

The work became
a body of software.

I spent my entire life improving processes.

ARTIFACTS began when I realised engineering itself was one of them.

What repeated, I automated.
What stood in the way, I rebuilt.

ARTIFACTS is the evidence that I never accepted the way things were as the way they had to stay.
